# ADR-198: A one-line composer, limits that offer another account, and removing accounts

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-06

## Context

A conversation stays on the account that started it, so one on the default account stopped at that account's limit with nothing to do but wait, and an account could be added but not removed. The failure card sat over the composer as a second box, and the notification settings offered six system sounds that no longer played.

## Decision

- **Composer.** One box with no divider, as in the reference app: attach, access and the context meter on the left, then the model, microphone and send on the right, on a single line.
- **Failure ledge (`account_card.rs`).** One line on the composer's top edge, like the queue. A usage limit says when it returns, read from the provider's words, and offers each other account next to "Try again".
- **Removing an account.** `remove_provider_account` deletes an app-managed account and its folder. It refuses the default account, which is the Mac's own CLI login, and any account a conversation still uses.
- **Sounds.** The host names the cue for each event (`cue`): permissions and questions take "input", pull requests "linked", the rest "finished". Settings keep each event's switch and "Listen"; the sound picker is gone. The stored sound fields remain unused by the window.
- **Caret.** The composer's caret blinks as the Mac's does, 0.58 s on in a 1.06 s cycle, restarting lit when it moves. The idle window redraws only at each flip while the composer has the keys and the window is active. The reference app's caret does not blink; this differs by the owner's choice.
- **Instructions.** A steer glides to the top of the view like a sent message: its stretch fills the view while the turn is anchored.
- **Color.** Dark glass now matches the reference app's tokens: the shell at 81% and the conversation under a near-black coat at 40%, where it had none. The sidebar opened over the workspace dims it by 40% and blurs only its own panel.
- **Project rail.** The footer, under the sidebar, shows five projects' robots with arrows that slide by one, and a swipe or a sideways wheel moves them too, settling on a whole tile, as the reference app's; the tooltip gives the name and the keys. Usage and Settings in the footer are icons with a tooltip. ⌘⇧← and ⌘⇧→ step between projects, unless the composer holds text, where they keep selecting to the line's ends. ⌥Tab goes back to the last project, and held it shows them by last use, which the pointer can also pick. ⌃Tab was already the conversation tabs' key.
- **Files.** The path bar over an opened file is gone; only Markdown keeps a bar, for Preview and Source.
- **Terminal tabs.** A terminal tab dragged from the panel onto the title bar becomes a title tab in any placement, with its shell still running; the panel opens a fresh shell if it would be left empty. A right click pins a title terminal as a small tile at the bar's far left, or closes it.
- **Terminal moves.** A terminal tab can be dragged onto the side panel or the bottom strip, where its shells rejoin the Terminal pane, or onto the conversation area, which splits with a pane for it, as the reference app's title terminals do. Its middle splits too: a terminal never replaces a pane and never becomes the active pane, because the composer and the shortcuts follow that one. It keeps its tab while it sits in a pane, and closing the pane only returns it there. A pinned terminal is a card in the deck of pinned tabs, no longer a separate tile.
- **Selection menu.** "Ask in the side chat" gives way to "Add to chat", which appends the selection to the composer's draft on its own paragraph, as the reference app does.
- **Second opinion.** The turn menu's action and its code are gone, which ends ADR-056 here. The host keeps `handoff_session`, which the handoff uses.
- **Composer height.** The box slides to the height of its text in 0.16 s, per conversation, with reduced motion cutting it. The caret's scroll aims at the height it is heading to, so the text does not jump while the box grows.
- **Tooltips.** They open 150 ms after the pointer arrives, centered above their element, or below it at the window's top, kept inside the window. The vendored gpui takes the element's bounds for that, in place of the mouse's position.
- **Title tabs.** A tab with an unsent draft is no longer dashed; this one departs from the reference app by choice. The close button shows only under the pointer, and the plus button has the reference app's plate, which brightens on hover.
- **Listening.** While the microphone records, the project's robot stands over the composer and hops with the voice, with fading bars on both sides. The window asks the host for the loudness (`dictation_level`, Mac only, as the other dictation commands) every 50 ms during a recording; a poll keeps these numbers away from the event stream that phones also receive.
- **Turn diffs.** A file row of a closed card now fetches the diff texts the transcript left out; only opening the whole card used to. Without them the row wrongly said the diff exceeded the storage limit. That message now shows only for a diff that was counted but too long to keep.
- **Order of projects.** The rail lists projects by newest activity. ⌥Tab starts from the current one, then those visited, then by newest activity.
- **Pressing twice.** ⌘Q, and Escape over a running agent, act only on a second press within 2 s; the first shows a small notice and otherwise does nothing. ⌘. and the Stop button stop at once.
- **Entering a project.** Coming back to a project's tabs shows the front one the way a tab click does, so its history loads.
- **Skills for agents.** The app's skills are linked into every installed provider's skills folder at start, and with `~/.claude/skills` into a named account's folder when its CLI starts; links never replace what's there, and those of removed skills are cleared. Claude and Codex are also told where the skills and the workspace's skill folders are, so a skill left out of a provider's listing can still be read. The listing's size limit is left alone: all descriptions together are about 42,000 characters.
- **Pages.** Claude's and Codex's system instructions, and the top of a thread's first prompt for every other provider, present or future, tell them to open and test pages in the built-in browser, and a page an agent opens brings up the Navegador pane for the conversation on screen.
- **Page under menus.** The web view still hides under a menu, since native views draw over GPUI, but a picture of the page stays in its place.
- **Narrow windows.** The side panel gives way so the conversation keeps 420 pt, down to 280 pt for the panel. Sent pictures become a sharp square copy, so a long or tall one stays inside its tile.
- **Narrow composer.** Below 440 pt the effort goes, below 390 pt the access level keeps only its icon, and the model's name truncates. The composer's picture copy is a 144 px centered crop, which fixes blurred long screenshots and tall ones spilling out of the tile.
- **Browser bar.** Tabs on top and the address below, with capture, the element picker and the device view inside the address field. Copy link, recording and the floating window moved to the `⋯` menu; recording and the floating window show in the field while on.
- **Project menu.** Scripts and Move left it. Open in editor lists the editors beside its row, on hover or click, as the reference app's submenus.
- **Page tabs.** Terminal and Pull requests tabs are laid out in the strip with the session tabs and drawn like them, with the same right-click rows, pinning into the deck included. The session tab under a page no longer reads as selected.
- **Project look.** A stage shows the robot on its colour, with tabs for colour, mascot and eyes; clicking it draws another mascot and eyes.
- **Keyboard after a page.** Clicking anywhere in the window takes the keys back from a web view, and a page that hides gives them up. AppKit left them with the page, so the composer could not be typed in, while dictation apps still could insert text.
- **Project actions.** As the reference app's: saved commands per project, kept in `native-actions.json`, run from a footer button the size of Usage and Settings. Its menu lists them, edits them beside the row and adds new ones; each runs in a terminal of its own, reused while it sits idle. The Scripts editor left the project menu with this; setup and finish scripts already saved still run.
- **Side panel's edge.** The resize grip is 12 pt wide and painted over the panel, whose controls used to take the clicks on its right half; the page leaves 6 pt at its left edge for it, because a web view takes every click over it.
- **Agents and the keyboard.** A browser tool run by an agent no longer leaves the keys with a page if they were with the app before it ran.
- **Buttons that answer.** One thumb, `segment_pill`, slides between the segments of the segmented controls in 0.22 s, and `Sinks` makes the main buttons sink a point and dim while pressed. gpui can't scale an element, so the press is a shift rather than the preview's scale. The rest of the app's one-off buttons adopt it as they are touched.
- **Result cards.** A finished or failed conversation's card stays until it is clicked, closed or the conversation is opened; it no longer leaves after six seconds.
- **Agents elsewhere.** While agents work in other projects, a pill over the project rail stacks their robots and counts them; it opens a list with each project's name and running time, and a row opens that conversation. The collapsed sidebar's peek brings the project rail along.
- **Instructions and the answer.** The stretch that fills the view from an instruction on now holds the answer and its cards too. It held only what came before them, which left a hole above the answer of a turn that received an instruction, until the conversation was reopened.
- **Settings and the peek.** Opening the settings closes a peeking sidebar, and neither it nor the agents' pill shows over their column. The project menu's row is now called Project settings.
- **Resources in the footer.** The bolt shows only when agents use a lot of CPU or memory or the Mac is under pressure, as the reference app's chip, left of Actions.
- **Browser bar by width.** The address keeps as many tools as the panel has room for, up to six, and the rest go to the `⋯` menu, whose rows now carry icons.
- **A narrow panel.** Below 440 pt the title bar shows pane icons without the chosen one's name; below 360 pt the diff leaves it too.
- **Message trail.** At rest its ticks take the 23 pt before the text, so it no longer covers a turn's tile; pointing at it widens it.
- **Editing a queued request.** It uses the composer's text model: selection, ⌘A, arrows, copy and paste, and clicks that place the caret or take a word or everything.
- **Scripts.** The editor left with the project menu's row, and its code is gone. Setup and On finish scripts already saved still run.
- **Radii.** The small and medium radii are 4 and 6 points, the same as the reference app's hover pills, rows, tabs and buttons, across the system.
- **Pictures in the viewer.** A picture takes its size from the stage's measured bounds (`fitted_picture`), since an image with no size of its own lays out at its natural size and looks zoomed in. The drawing's frame follows the same bounds.
- **Maximized panel.** The panel takes the exact width inside the card (`dock_room`): the conversation goes to zero, the divider and grip leave, and the browser page drops the grip's room.
- **Agents popup.** A short list, 300 points wide: the totals in the header, one row per conversation, and Parar in place of the figures under the pointer. Stopping a single process leaves the native UI; the host's action stays. Memory pressure shows only when it isn't normal.
- **Pairing dialog.** One column, 380 points wide, without the illustration: QR sized to the window, code, a ring for the time left, a sliding segmented control and buttons that wrap.
- **Command palette.** ⌘K follows the reference app: a taller field with its `esc` key, filter chips in the accent color (Tab cycles them), and rows that reach both edges with a short accent bar, a tile, a right-aligned detail and `↵`. One thumb, fill and bar together, glides between rows for keys and hover alike (`segment_pill::row_thumb`). Hover moves it only when the pointer really moves, so a list scrolling by keys keeps it. Files open in the Files pane instead of only showing the pane.
- **Removing a project.** The window lets go of a project only once the host confirms, and asks to stop running conversations first, since the host refuses them. Its slot in `projects` stays, because others are known by index; `project_order` says who is listed, so the rail, ⌥Tab, ⌘⇧ arrows and ⌘K read that, and its conversations leave with it.
- **Agents pill with the sidebar closed.** Over the rail's column it floated on the composer, so it joins the footer's row, smaller and without a shadow, and only its list floats, above it.
- **Account names.** "Conta padrão" meant the Mac's own Claude Code login whatever new conversations used. That login is now "Conta do Terminal"; the tag "Conta padrão" follows the selected account, whose card comes first.
- **Conversation hover card.** Follows the reference app: 260 points wide, the model on the left and the state on the right in its color (needs approval, working, failed, done, or the age), the whole title, and a ruled section with the branch and the folder or worktree.
- **Keys and closed fields.** A settings field or commit message whose screen closed no longer keeps the typing, which left the composer dead after finishing a pairing.
- **Connecting a phone.** The phone's screen opens on a Scan QR button, using the camera (`expo-camera`), with typing a link or address and code behind it; `pairingFromLink` reads either kind of link. On the Mac the dialog is the QR alone, and a device that joins gets a plain confirmation: naming moved to the device list.
- **Tailscale is gone.** Other devices reach a computer only through its Cloudflare Tunnel (ADR-155), supersedes ADR-082 and the tailnet parts of ADR-080, ADR-131 and ADR-132. The host answers loopback peers alone and listens on loopback by default; `--https-port`, `tailscale serve` and pages paired across `*.ts.net` left with it. Connections shows the public route's address, and the browser way of pairing is gone.
- **A press on a browser menu's button.** The scrim closes the menu on press and the button gets the click, so the same button no longer opens it again.
- **Selecting any answer text.** Every text beat of a turn takes selection, also those between steps or while the agent works; only the last settled one did. A selection stays within one beat.
- **The VPS without Tailscale.** The `sirus` container owns its network on the isolated `sirus` bridge, `sirus-cloudflared` shares it, and `--url` names `https://vps.siruscode.com`. The `sirus-tailscale` container and its state are gone.
- **Connector sign-in.** The host opens the sign-in page in the default browser on the Mac; it had kept the phone's refusal since the Tauri window left. Result cards draw over Settings and Usage, whose pages hid them.
- **The robot over the composer.** It walks on top of the whole stack, so a queued instruction or a card sits under it.
- **Renaming a row.** The text starts at the left and only an overflow keeps its end in view. ⌘A selects the name, which the next key replaces, and ⌘C, ⌘X and ⌘V work in the field.
- **Panels per project.** Each project keeps its own side panel and bottom terminal (open, panes, maximized); a project not yet opened in this session starts with them closed. The width stays shared.
- **Chips open files.** A file or folder chip in a reply or a step opens in the Files pane when it names something real inside the conversation's folder; anything else stays plain (`file_chips.rs`). A bare name goes to the shallowest match in the folder's file list.
- **Menu switches and the agents pill.** The sidebar menu's switches are Settings' switch in the accent color; the agents pill has a session tab's corners. The Environment popup's rows slide open and shut.
- **Phone camera pin.** `expo-camera` stays at 58.0.12: 58.0.13 and 58.0.14 ship a prebuilt framework that needs a newer `ExpoModulesCore` and crash at launch.
- **Compaction choices.** "Auto-compactar depois de" passes Claude's `autoCompactWindow` (100,000 to 1,000,000, empty by default). A heavy conversation idle past its cache now means 200,000 tokens, and a chip beside send says "Compactar" with the tokens, or "Manter" after a click, for one message.
- **Skills by project.** Claude's skill listing has a budget of 1% of the context window; past it skills arrive as a bare name and are not found. `skill_scope.rs` moves skills of a stack the project doesn't use to `user-invocable-only`, from its dependencies and `AGENTS.md`/`CLAUDE.md`; a `Skills: a, b` line keeps those. They stay callable from the composer. Skills off in Settings are now off for Claude too.
- **Shared MCP route.** Claude conversations reach the browser and computer servers over one loopback HTTP route in the host (`mcp_http.rs`) instead of two child processes each; other providers keep their children. A delegated task follows the person's `subagentPromptCacheTtl`.
- **Conversations in memory.** Sixteen transcripts stay loaded besides open tabs and work under way; the oldest reloads when shown.
- **Measuring.** `native-spike/scripts/measure.py` takes a comparable reading of CPU and memory and can fail on a budget.
- **The phone's turn matches the Mac's.** A reply opens with the provider, the model and the time; a run of steps is one group with a tile, a title and rows on a rail, with the command's purpose and chips, open while it works. A sent message leaves the composer at once (and returns if the send fails), the keyboard closes, and the message rises to the top while the reply grows under it. The add sheet is one group, every row with an icon.
- **Upright photos.** Thumbnails follow a photo's EXIF orientation; an iPhone stores a portrait sideways.
- **Code wraps.** The Files pane's editor wraps long lines by default, under the line's indent and with one number per line; a switch in the tab strip turns that off and is remembered. Up and Down still move by whole lines.
- **Folders slide.** A folder in the Files tree slides open and shut, its chevron turning with it.
- **Accounts share a row.** Connectors from the same catalog entry show as one row with up to three account chips, each opening that account's menu. A single button follows: "+" connects another, or "+N" opens a popup with every account, those waiting to sign in first and a search from eight accounts on.
- **The phone's tiles.** A reply's header and its work runs wear the Mac's tiles (`Tile.tsx`), the header names the model as the Mac does and opens the folded work, and a sent message retries its rise until it reaches the top.
- **Antigravity over ACP.** Antigravity runs through `acp_cli.rs` (`agy acp`) instead of print mode: live permission requests by access mode, planning, exact resume and cancellation. It has no mode that skips asking, so Full access answers for the person; the reasoning level comes from the model's name.
- **Visuals stay above later instructions.** A visual records how many instructions the reply had taken, and stays above any that come later.
- **The selection menu.** It is smaller and its hover slides.
- **Failures and compaction.** A failure is a toast at the top of the window that leaves after eight seconds, no longer a line in the footer, and a host message the app has a sentence for reads in Portuguese: the host stays in English, and the whole fixed sentences, 556 of its error messages, are looked up from the English side of the table; messages built at run time, with a path or Git's own error in them, stay in English. A queued message offers no Instruir while the conversation compacts, because it goes out behind the compaction anyway. Beside "Compactar e enviar" the chip reads "Manter 735K", the other choice, since "Compactar 735K" looked like a button that compacts and did the opposite.
- **Signing.** A local build is signed with the Apple Development identity. With the self-signed one the Keychain tied each item to that build's hash, so every new build asked for the password again; with a team identity an item is tied to the team, tested with a throwaway item that a rebuilt binary read with prompts turned off. The change of identity makes macOS ask once more for each item, and for the permissions it ties to the signature, such as the microphone.
- **The subagent card.** The title says "3 subagentes" with a pip per subagent and "1 de 3", no longer the person's message cut short, which sits just above it, and one subagent has no header at all. A row shows what the agent reports it is doing while it runs and nothing once it is done, because "Running cargo test" would read as still happening. Its buttons take the place of the step count instead of floating over it: the frosted panel they sat on showed through at zero opacity and cut the elapsed time. Each button has a name, the hover slides, and "segundo plano" wears layers instead of a bolt.
- **A PDF outside the project.** A chip naming a PDF elsewhere, such as one an agent saved in Downloads, opens in the reader. The app reads it on that click, because the host only reads inside the conversation's folder; any other file outside the folder still opens nothing.
- **Command chips and the live header.** A redirect no longer makes a chip: `2>/dev/null` was a folder called `null`. "Rodando 3 comandos" and the rest of the live header are in Portuguese.
- **Segmented controls wear the accent.** A control that changes what the screen shows has a solid accent thumb: Custo, Tokens and Limites, Preview and Código, the project's look, the first run's theme. One among the many choices of a Settings page has an 18% accent wash, because a page of solid thumbs competes with itself and with send. The phone's control is no longer the system's: its thumb slides as the Mac's does.
- **Dictation on the phone.** The image picker's `microphonePermission: false` deleted the microphone's usage string the speech plugin had written, and iOS closes an app that opens the microphone without it, so that plugin now runs last. While it records, the project's robot stands over the composer and hops with the voice, drawn from the same shapes as the Mac's.
- **The working pill counts what is done.** "0/1" meant none running, which read as none finished; it now says "1 de 1", as the subagent card does.
- **Settings are real, one at a time.** A confirmation opens above the Settings page, which used to cover it; the last row of a card has no rule of its own, which ran past the rounded corners; and "Provedor padrão" is a choice that a new session now follows, among the providers installed and on. Twenty-nine settings still save and change nothing on the Mac, left from the removed web app.
- **From Monocode and T3 Code.** Text pasted from 32 KiB up becomes a file, and ⇧⌘V keeps it as text. A click on a web address in the terminal opens it, and a drag still selects it. The middle button closes a tab. Names sort as a person reads them. Autosave, off until its button in the file pane is on, waits a second after typing and stops when another program changed the file. "Corrigir o último" amends a commit no remote has, and an empty title keeps its message. A project can be silenced for 1, 4 or 8 hours or until turned back on, which hides its cards, sounds and badge and leaves what it left unread. The history draws its lanes, branches and tags.
- **Live Activity.** A working conversation shows on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, with its running time. The phone has no server push, because the owner's Apple account is free, so a suspended app can't update it: after ten minutes it says to open Sirus, and only then can it say "Terminou". The extension's sources are in `mobile/targets/live-activity`, and `mobile/scripts/add-live-activity-target.rb` adds it to the generated project.
- **Monocode round two (2026-11-08).** A connector sign-in can be cancelled and waits two minutes; a new one replaces the old (`connectors.rs` `CancelSignIn`). Files open in an italic preview tab, can show what Git ignores (dimmed), and SCSS, Less, Zig and `.env` are coloured. The window reopens where it was (`window_place.rs`). The composer takes `/stage` and `/commit [message]`; without a message the title comes from `commit_title_action` (`git_slash.rs`). Each checkout row in the project menu starts a conversation there (`shareWorktreeOf`). An issue or PR named in the first message becomes a chip in the composer bar (`issue_ref.rs`, `issue_chip.rs`). ⌘/⇧ pick several conversations for Abrir, Fixar or Excluir. "Editar" on the last message drops it and its reply and refills the composer; Codex, Claude and OpenCode restart their thread primed like a fork (`turn_edit.rs`). Text fields and the terminal have a right-click edit menu. A stalled main thread is logged to `~/Library/Logs/Sirus/freezes.log`, sampled past 4 s (`freeze_watch.rs`); five minutes in the background drop decoded images and return free pages (`memory_trim.rs`, `App::trim_images` in the vendored GPUI). ⌥Space or the menu bar icon opens the quick composer (`quick_composer.rs`). ⌥Tab shows a miniature of each project's latest conversation. Dragging on the empty screen leaves fading ink. GitLab (`glab`) and Bitbucket (REST, token in the Keychain) show and create the branch's MR/PR with checks (`forge.rs`). A secret card can answer from 1Password through `op`; the window sees only titles and field names (`onepassword.rs`). Settings: 15 dead options now work and 19 were removed; "Consumo na barra inferior" picks up to two providers; "Manter o Mac acordado enquanto um agente trabalha" and "Composer rápido" are switches, both on by default.
- **Monocode round three (2026-11-08).** The quick composer's global hotkey is ⌃⌥Space and is recorded under Atalhos → Globais; recording warns about macOS shortcuts, another app's registration and known apps that listen to the keyboard (`hotkey_conflicts.rs`). The Dock icon is one of six halftone-bust looks drawn at runtime, one in the accent colour; the bundle icon is the face (`app_icon.rs`). Settings search is a real field that indexes every page from the data the pages render, in Portuguese and English, and jumps to the row with a flash (`settings_search.rs`). Computer use may drive Sirus itself only when "Permitir que agentes controlem o Sirus" is on (`computer_mcp::set_self_allowed`), because an agent that clicks Sirus can approve its own requests. ⌘F is an opaque pill, gives typing back to the composer on click, and counts and marks exact occurrences. The jump to the latest turn reaches the end in about 250 ms from any distance (`Glide::follow_fast`), and the turn rail lights the turn in view. Alterações is history only, with the graph in accent rails; the top bar's +N −N opens the review view (message, Commit ▾ with Commit e Push, Push, Criar PR, Corrigir o último; ⌘↩ only; discard asks first). ⌥Tab cards turn the sidebar's spinner while a project works. The context popup is a narrow pill with Compactar under it; the usage popup has a tab per provider and each provider in the bottom bar opens its own. A queued message is always one line. A new conversation picks where it works under the composer (`workspace_choice.rs`), only before its first message, as Monocode does. A maximized right panel keeps the active conversation in a floating pill that opens into a drawer with the real transcript and composer, ⌘L, and opens itself for an approval (`conversation_balloon.rs`).
- **Turn actions.** The `⋯` menu is gone. A reply's row reads Copy, Handoff, Side chat, Fork, with Undo after them. Try again left it: it now sits under the sent message, only on the last turn and only after a failure.
- **Edge fades.** The transcript's fades are now an alpha mask around the list. The gradients drawn in the conversation's color showed as dark bands once the conversation became translucent black.
- **Inline badges.** A link in the composer or a sent message is a badge with the site's favicon and its domain (the address in a tooltip), an `@file` the file's icon and name, and a `/command` or skill a badge in the accent colour (`inline_badges.rs`). The text stays the plain string; only the drawing changes. GPUI's `StyledText` has no inline boxes, so the text is laid out with a gap of non-breaking spaces where each badge goes (word characters, so a badge wraps as one unit) and the badge is painted over the gap at the position the layout reports. `Shown` maps byte indexes between the two, so the caret, the selection and a click never land inside a badge, Backspace after one deletes the whole token, and a copy over a sent message gives the plain text. Favicons load off the main thread from Google's favicon service or the site's `/favicon.ico`, and are kept in memory and under `~/Library/Caches/com.siruscode.native/favicons`; a globe stands in until one arrives.
- **A Git split button in the top bar.** It replaces ADR-148's header Pull button and sits before the +N -N numbers: no fill or border at rest, 30 px high, a left half that does the one thing the branch needs (Puxar when behind, Enviar when ahead and clean, Commit e enviar with changes, Sincronizar when both sides have commits, a bare cloud that opens the menu when there is nothing to do) and a chevron that opens "Ações do Git" (Commit, Commit e enviar, Puxar, Enviar, Criar PR). Rows that cannot run are dimmed and say why on hover; the footer warns in amber when behind and otherwise says when GitHub was last asked. Something to pull wins over local changes, since a push would be refused. The host fetches (`git_incoming`) every 5 minutes while the window is visible, when it becomes active, when the menu opens and after a commit, pull or push, and never more than once per 30 s per checkout (it was 15 s). `git_sync_pull { sessionId, rebase }` pulls with the tracked local changes stashed and put back (`--ff-only`, or `--rebase=true` for Sincronizar, then a push); on a conflict it aborts the rebase or merge, restores the changes and answers `conflict { files }`, which the window shows as "Não deu para puxar". Untracked files stay where they are, because `stash -u` does nothing under the `--literal-pathspecs` every native Git call carries. A commit stages what the host lets move, asks `commit_title_action` for the message and commits against that index, as the review view does.
- **Quote blocks that hold their own reply.** "Adicionar ao chat" (menu or ⇧⌘L) puts the selected passage in the composer as a bordered block: a 3 px accent bar, the source ("Resposta do <modelo>", "Sua mensagem" or `path:from-to`), the quote clamped to two lines with a ✕, and under it an editable reply marked with ↳ that tints the border while it has the caret. The block goes after the one whose reply holds the caret, else after the current line, and a free line always sits below the last block. ↓ or → at the end of a line moves on, Enter on an empty last line leaves the block, Backspace in an empty reply removes it, and a click on empty space focuses the last free line. A `Draft` with quotes is a list of segments; the active segment's text lives in `Draft::text`, so every key and IME call is unchanged, and a plain draft has no segments. The agent receives, and the draft is saved as, a markdown blockquote `> (source) text`, a blank line and the reply as `↳ reply` (continuation lines indented by two spaces), with free text between, all separated by blank lines; the sent message parses it back into cards and a click on a quote scrolls to the turn it came from (`composer_quote.rs`).
- **The agent's pointer.** When an agent clicks, types, scrolls or drags in the Navegador pane, or clicks, types, presses a key or scrolls in another Mac app through computer use, an arrow in the provider's colour (Claude #d97757, Codex #e5e5e5, Gemini/Antigravity #8fb4f5, otherwise the accent) glides to the target in 0.6 s on cubic-bezier(.3, .7, .2, 1) with a short fading trail of dots, a pill beside it shows the provider's mark and name, each click rings from 10 to 46 px in 0.6 s, a scroll shows two chevrons under the arrow, typing rests the arrow at the field, and it fades out 3 s after the last action; with Reduce motion there is no glide or trail, only a short fade at the new place.
